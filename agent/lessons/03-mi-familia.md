# Lección 3: Mi familia

## Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson, the learner can:

- Talk about their family.
- Ask other people about their family.
- Describe people.
- Use the verb **tener**.
- Use **mi**, **tu**, and **su**.
- Use masculine and feminine adjective agreement.

## Useful sentence patterns

- **Este es mi padre.**  
  This is my father.

- **Esta es mi hermana.**  
  This is my sister.

- **Tengo dos hermanos.**  
  I have two siblings / brothers.

- **¿Tienes hermanos?**  
  Do you have siblings?

- **Se llama...**  
  His / her name is...

- **Es...**  
  He / she is...

- **Tiene...**  
  He / she has...

## Useful questions

- **¿Quién es?**  
  Who is he / she?

- **¿Cómo se llama?**  
  What is his / her name?

- **¿Cómo es?**  
  What is he / she like?

- **¿Tiene mascotas?**  
  Does he / she have pets?

## Example description

> Esta es mi madre. Se llama Susan. Es alta, morena y muy simpática. Tiene un perro.

This is my mother. Her name is Susan. She is tall, dark-haired, and very friendly. She has a dog.

## Example dialogue

**Ana:** Hola. ¿Qué tal?

**Carlos:** Muy bien. ¿Y tú?

**Ana:** Muy bien, gracias. ¿Tienes hermanos?

**Carlos:** Sí. Tengo una hermana y un hermano.

**Ana:** ¿Cómo se llaman?

**Carlos:** Mi hermana se llama Sofía y mi hermano se llama Diego.

**Ana:** ¿Cómo son?

**Carlos:** Sofía es alta, rubia y simpática. Diego es bajo, moreno y divertido.

**Ana:** Tengo una hermana. Se llama Marta. Es inteligente y amable.

## Speaking challenge

Introduce one important person in your life.

Include:

- Who the person is.
- Their name.
- What they are like.
- Whether they have pets.

Use questions such as:

- **¿Quién es?**
- **¿Cómo se llama?**
- **¿Cómo es?**
- **¿Tiene mascotas?**

# Roleplay

### Scenario

The learner and the bot are classmates talking about important people in their lives.

### Round 1

The bot asks the learner about one family member, friend, or partner.

The learner should practise:

- **Este es... / Esta es...**
- **Se llama...**
- **Es...**
- **Tiene...**
- **Tengo...**

The bot should naturally ask:

- **¿Quién es?**
- **¿Cómo se llama?**
- **¿Cómo es?**
- **¿Tiene mascotas?**

Round 1 ends only after the learner has said who the person is, given their
name, described them, and said whether they have pets. Then immediately
switch roles and begin Round 2.

### Round 2

The roles switch.

The bot introduces a fictional family member or friend. The learner asks questions about that person.

The bot should answer using only vocabulary and grammar from this lesson.

Round 2 ends only after the learner has asked all four questions and the bot
has answered them. Only then is the roleplay complete.
