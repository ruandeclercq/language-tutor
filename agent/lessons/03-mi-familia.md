# Lección 3: Mi familia

## Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson, the learner can:

- Talk about their family.
- Ask other people about their family.
- Describe people.
- Use the verb **tener**.
- Use **mi**, **tu**, and **su**.
- Use masculine and feminine adjective agreement.

## Grammar

### The verb `tener`

| Subject | Form |
|---|---|
| yo | tengo |
| tú | tienes |
| él / ella | tiene |
| nosotros | tenemos |
| ellos / ellas | tienen |

Examples:

- **Ana tiene una hermana.**
- **Tenemos un perro.**

### Possessive adjectives

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| my | mi / mis |
| your | tu / tus |
| his / her | su / sus |

## Useful sentence patterns

- **Este es mi padre.**  
  This is my father.

- **Esta es mi hermana.**  
  This is my sister.

- **Tengo dos hermanos.**  
  I have two siblings / brothers.

- **¿Tienes hermanos?**  
  Do you have siblings?

- **Se llama...**  
  His / her name is...

- **Es...**  
  He / she is...

- **Tiene...**  
  He / she has...

- **No tiene mascotas.**
  He / she does not have pets.
- **No tengo...**
  I do not have...

- **Vive...**
  He / she lives...

## Useful questions

- **¿Quién es?**  
  Who is he / she?

- **¿Cómo se llama?**  
  What is his / her name?

- **¿Cómo es?**  
  What is he / she like?

- **¿Tiene mascotas?**  
  Does he / she have pets?
- **¿Cuántos hermanos tienes?**
  How many siblings do you have?
- **¿Cómo se llama tu madre?**
  What is your mother's name?
- **¿Cómo se llama tu padre?**
  What is your father's name?

## Example description

> Esta es mi madre. Se llama Susan. Es alta, morena y muy simpática. Tiene un perro.

This is my mother. Her name is Susan. She is tall, dark-haired, and very friendly. She has a dog.

## Example dialogue

**Ana:** Hola. ¿Qué tal?

**Carlos:** Muy bien. ¿Y tú?

**Ana:** Muy bien, gracias. ¿Tienes hermanos?

**Carlos:** Sí. Tengo una hermana y un hermano.

**Ana:** ¿Cómo se llaman?

**Carlos:** Mi hermana se llama Sofía y mi hermano se llama Diego.

**Ana:** ¿Cómo son?

**Carlos:** Sofía es alta, rubia y simpática. Diego es bajo, moreno y divertido.

**Ana:** Tengo una hermana. Se llama Marta. Es inteligente y amable.

# Roleplay

## Round 1

The tutor asks the student about one family member, friend, or partner.

Choose one relationship at random from the vocabulary. Do not always begin
with `¿Tienes hermanos?`; start with a question about the chosen relationship.

The student should practise:

- **Este es... / Esta es...**
- **Se llama...**
- **Es...**
- **Tiene...**
- **Tengo...**

The tutor asks:

- **¿Quién es?**
- **¿Cómo se llama?**
- **¿Cómo es?**
- **¿Tiene mascotas?**

Round 1 ends only after the student has said who the person is, given their
name, described them, and said whether they have pets. Then immediately
switch roles and begin Round 2.

## Round 2

The roles switch.

The tutor introduces a fictional family member or friend. The student asks
questions about that person.

The tutor answers using only vocabulary and grammar from this lesson.

For each roleplay, invent different details. Vary the person's relationship,
name, description, siblings, and pets using only material from this lesson.
For pets, use either `Tiene un perro.` or `No tiene mascotas.`

Round 2 ends only after the student has asked all four questions and the tutor
has answered them. Only then is the roleplay complete.
