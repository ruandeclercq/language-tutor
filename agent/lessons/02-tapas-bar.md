# Restaurant language

## Waiter

- Hola, buenas tardes.
- ¿Qué le puedo ofrecer?
- ¿Qué le pongo?
- Enseguida.
- Claro.
- Lo siento. No hay...

## Customer

- Una cerveza, por favor.
- Un poco de queso, por favor.
- Muchas gracias.
- Vale.
- Entonces...

# Dialogue

## Example

**Camarero**

Hola, buenas tardes.

**Cliente**

Hola.

**Camarero**

¿Qué le puedo ofrecer?

**Cliente**

Una cerveza y unas patatas bravas, por favor.

**Camarero**

Enseguida.

**Cliente**

Muchas gracias.

# Roleplay

Goal:

Order food in a tapas bar.

First round: the tutor is the waiter and the student is the customer.

The customer orders:

- one drink
- two foods

The waiter randomly makes one menu item unavailable.

When the student is the waiter, tell them to make one menu item unavailable.

Example:

> Lo siento. No hay queso.

The customer should order something else.

The round ends only after the customer has successfully ordered one drink and two available foods.

Completing the first round does not complete the lesson. Immediately switch roles and complete the second round. The lesson ends only after both rounds are complete.


# Challenge

The student should be able to:

- describe what people do in a tapas bar
- say what they like and dislike
- explain why
- order food politely
- react when something is unavailable

---

# Success criteria

The lesson is complete when the student can hold a 2-3 minute conversation in a tapas bar without English.
